01 / PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Accountability Gap

Over 1,000 individuals appear across 12 DOJ Epstein datasets (~300 GB). The records exist — but no structured system has ever answered the central question: who actually faced consequences?

⚖

The Justice Gap

Of all named individuals, fewer than 10% faced any criminal charges. Most walked away despite documented evidence of involvement.

📁

The Data Problem

The DOJ released 12 unstructured PDF datasets with no searchable index, no accountability tracker, and no NLP analysis — until now.

🔬

Our Contribution

We built the first NLP pipeline over real DOJ Epstein files — extracting entities, classifying consequences, and enabling RAG citation at document level.

NOVELTY VS PRIOR WORK

Unlike epsteinoverview.com (static, manually-curated, no ML), Paper Trail performs live inference over 961 real DOJ PDFs downloaded via ZIP range-request extraction — a technique that avoids the 300 GB full download. Every AI answer cites the specific EFTA document ID it drew from.
